How SignBridge works

No special equipment, no training needed. Just your hands, a camera, and a little magic.

1
📷
Open Camera

Open SignBridge in any browser and allow camera access. Works on phones, tablets, and laptops — no app download required.

2
🤙
Make a Sign

Place your hands in view and sign naturally. Our AI model tracks 21 hand landmarks per hand at 30 frames per second.

3
⚡
Instant Translation

Watch the translation appear in real-time. Context-aware AI strings signs into complete, natural sentences — not just isolated words.

4
🔊
Speak or Share

Send the text, have it spoken aloud, copy it to another app, or export a transcript. Your words, your way.

🌟 Everything you need

Packed with friendly features

Designed for daily life — fast, private, and truly accessible for everyone.

⚡
Real-Time Detection

Sub-100ms latency using on-device AI. Your signs are recognized before you even finish making them, creating a truly fluid conversation flow.

🧠
Context-Aware AI

Goes beyond gesture-by-gesture detection. Our language model understands full sentences, grammar rules, and common idioms in sign language.

🔒
100% Private

All processing happens on your device. Your camera feed is never sent to our servers. What you sign stays with you, always.

🌍
Sign Languages

Supports ASL, BSL, ISL, Auslan, LSF, DGS and more. Easily switch between languages with a single tap from the language selector.

🔊
Voice Output

Translated text can be spoken aloud instantly using natural-sounding text-to-speech in over 40 voices and accents. Great for face-to-face conversations.

📚
Learn Mode

Practice and improve your sign language skills with guided exercises, instant feedback on your form, and a full ASL/BSL reference dictionary.
